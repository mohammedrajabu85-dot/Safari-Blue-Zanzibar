## 🔹 What is Safari Blue?
Safari Blue is one of the most famous sea excursions in Zanzibar. It includes:
- Snorkeling with colorful tropical fish  
- Swimming in the Blue Lagoon  
- Dolphin spotting  
- Sandbank relaxation  
- Seafood lunch buffet  
- Traditional dhow sailing experience
